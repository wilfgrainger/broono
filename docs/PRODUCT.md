# Broono: 99 Nights

## Product direction

Broono is an original mobile-first survival game inspired by the accessible day/night rhythm of social survival games. Broono is a zombie dog: strange-looking, loyal and trying to remain a good boy.

The player gathers wood and salvage during daylight, maintains a protective warm light, explores a changing Wildwood, rescues lost animals and survives escalating night creatures through night 99.

## Originality guardrail

We may reuse genre mechanics: day/night survival, gathering, crafting, camp defence, procedural exploration and cooperative play. We do not copy another game's title, characters, creature designs, maps, UI, dialogue, audio, artwork, code, exact balance tables or story-specific rescue targets.

## First playable slice

- Top-down keyboard and touch movement.
- Original procedural woodland presentation with no external art dependency.
- Day/night timer and 99-night progression.
- Wood and scrap gathering.
- Warm-light fuel loop.
- Night enemy spawning and health pressure.
- Google login integration point, guest play, Worker-authenticated save API and leaderboard schema.
