# Product Vision & Aesthetics
- **Core Concept**: Mobile-first, Tamagotchi-style pet simulation game.
- **Art Style**: Bright, playful fusion of pastel pixel art and modern cartoon aesthetics.
- **Nurture Loop**: Daily check-ins, hatching egg, managing vitals (Hunger, Hydration, Temperature, Happiness).
- **Mini-Games**: Basic crosswords and word puzzles to earn coins.
- **Economy**: Coins earned through care and mini-games; premium shop unlocks at 1,000 coins.
- **Social**: Leaderboards (Global, Country, Friends & Family).
